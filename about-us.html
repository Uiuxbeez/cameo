ABOUT CAMEO ADVERTISING

Complete Signage Solutions for Businesses Across the UAE

Cameo Advertising is a premier provider of complete signage advertising solutions for corporate identities and businesses across the UAE. Our creativity and artistry showcase the identity of every brand.

Established in 2022, Cameo Advertising brings together a solid, experienced team of more than 30 employees. We design and manufacture outdoor and indoor signage, architectural 3D signage, wayfinding signs, brand activations, kiosks and exhibition stands, acrylic and metal fabrication, fleet branding, digital screens and interactive displays.

We offer convenient installation and project management services for your business. Our team works to maximise your visibility, guiding you from project booking through installation and helping you fulfil your signage needs.

Whether it is spectacular outdoor signage or a warm, welcoming indoor sign, we bring your vision to life. Careful time management and close cooperation keep customer satisfaction at the heart of our work.
